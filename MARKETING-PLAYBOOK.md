# Water & Ink Studio: 30-day organic launch

## The one path every post should take

**A compelling image or process clip → Studio Notes on waterandinkstudio.netlify.app → Etsy for originals or Printify for wearable work.**

Use one call to action per post. Do not put all three destinations in every caption.

| What you are showing | Destination | Call to action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-of-one painting or drawing | Etsy | “View the available original in the Etsy shop.” |
| Shirt, tote, or everyday object | Printify | “See the wearable work in the print shop.” |
| Process, studio detail, personal story | Studio Notes | “Join Studio Notes for first look at new work.” |

## Content pillars

1. **The making** — ink spreading, brushwork, paper, a piece taking shape.
2. **The small world** — the place, object, memory, or observation inside a work.
3. **The object** — framed original, close-up texture, shirt, tote, or print in use.
4. **The artist’s eye** — why an imperfect line, open space, or quiet subject matters.

## Posting rhythm

Post five times a week on the social channels you actually enjoy. Turn each finished post into two Pinterest Pins: one close crop and one full composition, each linked to the relevant destination. Reuse the idea—not the exact caption.

### Week 1 — Introduce the world

1. **Reel / short video:** blank page to finished work. “A small world, held in ink. This is how the first marks became *[title]*. Studio Notes gets first look at what comes next.”
2. **Carousel:** three close-ups of one original. “The work begins where the line wobbles a little. Details from *[title]*.”
3. **Photo:** you / the worktable / materials. “Water, ink, brush, paper, and enough quiet to notice what is already there.”
4. **Comparison post:** two works. “Which quiet place would you step into: I or II?”
5. **Shop post:** an available original. “*[title]* is a one-of-one original, now in the Etsy shop.”

### Week 2 — Let people into the process

6. Brush close-up: “The dry-brush marks stay. They are where the work begins.”
7. A source photo versus the finished work: “A corner I noticed twice.”
8. Packaging / scale / framed piece: “Made by hand, made to live with.”
9. Printify piece worn or styled: “A studio mark that comes with you.”
10. Sketchbook page: “A note from the sketchbook. Studio Notes gets the next one first.”

### Week 3 — Create a reason to follow now

11. Announce a small, dated release: “Five new originals arrive [date]. Studio Notes sees them first.”
12. Detail of a release piece: “First of five: *[title]*.”
13. Story post: “Why I keep returning to pine, stone, and water.”
14. Q&A post: “Ask me anything about ink, paper, or this piece.”
15. Print gift angle: “For the person who wants their everyday things a little stranger and quieter.”

### Week 4 — Release and invite conversation

16. Release-day studio video: “The new originals are live.”
17. One artwork, one story: “This piece began with…”
18. Customer-facing question: “What room would this live in?”
19. Best-performing process clip, recut: “Watch the ink decide where it wants to go.”
20. Monthly reflection: “Five things I noticed while making this month’s work.”

Repeat the cycle with new work. The goal is consistency and recognizable point of view, not a different campaign every week.

## Weekly measurement

Every Sunday, record new Studio Notes signups, purchase inquiries, Etsy visits/favorites/orders, Printify visits/orders, and the top three posts by saves, shares, and link clicks. Make more versions of the post with the strongest combination of saves and link clicks.
